Common Digital Frauds in India

Learn to identify and protect yourself from these prevalent scams

UPI Fraud

Scammers exploit UPI apps to trick users into authorizing payments. They may pose as merchants, bank officials, or create fake payment collection requests.

Warning Signs:

  • Requests to share UPI PIN
  • Urgency to scan QR codes to "receive" money
  • Calls claiming your UPI account will be blocked

KYC Scam

Fraudsters claim your bank/wallet KYC is incomplete and needs immediate updating to avoid account suspension. They use this pretext to steal your credentials or money.

Warning Signs:

  • Threatening messages about account suspension
  • Requests to download remote access apps
  • Pressure to make "verification payments"

Job Scams

Fake job postings that require payments for "registration," "training," or "background checks." These often promise high salaries for minimal work and target desperate job seekers.

Warning Signs:

  • Too-good-to-be-true salary offers
  • Upfront fees before job confirmation
  • Vague job descriptions with poor grammar

Fake Investment Apps

Fraudulent apps that promise extraordinary returns on investments. After initial small returns to build trust, larger investments disappear along with the scammers.

Warning Signs:

  • Guaranteed high returns with no risk
  • Pressure to invite friends and family
  • Apps not listed on official app stores

SMS Scams

Deceptive text messages with malicious links that either install malware or lead to phishing sites designed to steal your personal and financial information.

Warning Signs:

  • Messages about prizes or unexpected money
  • Shortened or suspicious URLs
  • Requests to update account information via SMS

Fake Government Schemes

Fraudsters create fictitious government benefit programs, asking victims to pay a small "processing fee" to receive substantial government assistance.

Warning Signs:

  • Schemes not announced on official government websites
  • Registration fees for government benefits
  • Collection of unnecessary personal information

Credit Card Skimming

Physical or digital methods to steal credit card information from the magnetic strip. Common at ATMs, petrol pumps, and compromised payment websites.

Warning Signs:

  • Loose or unusual card readers at ATMs
  • Unsecured payment websites
  • Unexpected card transactions in your statement

Romance Scams

Scammers create fake online identities to establish romantic relationships, then exploit emotional connections to request money for emergencies or travel expenses.

Warning Signs:

  • Reluctance to video chat or meet in person
  • Rapid emotional attachment
  • Requests for financial assistance

General Prevention Tips

Never Share OTPs

No legitimate organization will ever ask for your OTP, password, or PIN over phone or email.

Verify Before Acting

Always independently verify urgent requests by contacting the organization through official channels.

Use Official Apps

Download financial apps only from official app stores after verifying the developer.

Monitor Accounts

Regularly check your bank and credit card statements for unauthorized transactions.
